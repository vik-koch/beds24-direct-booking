# Your guide to Zirbe

> :info: Firn & Farn is made up: this page is a demo, and the apartment and its fittings are
> examples.

Welcome to Zirbe. This page is for your stay: the QR code by the door leads here. If something does
not work, call us at [+43 000 0000000](tel:+430000000000).

## Heating

The floor is heated, and the dial by the door sets the room's warmth: 3 is about 21 °C. The floor
takes an hour or two to follow a change, so turn it a step at a time. Please keep the window closed
while the heating is on; to air the room, open it wide for five minutes.

## Kitchenette

The hob is induction: the pans in the cupboard below work on it, and others may not. It switches off
by itself when a pan is lifted for longer than a minute. The kettle and the coffee maker share the
socket under the shelf.

## Wi-Fi

The network's name and password are on the card inside the wardrobe door.

## Waste

Sort it into the bins under the sink: paper, packaging and the rest. Glass and anything left over go
to the bins beside the parking spaces, under the roof.

## Before you leave

- Sweep the floor and do the dishes; the final cleaning is ours.
- Turn the heating dial to 1 and close the window.
- Leave the keys on the table and pull the door shut behind you.
