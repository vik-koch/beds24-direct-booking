# Getting to Zirbe

> Firn & Farn is made up: this page is a demo, and the house and its apartment are examples.

Zirbe is on the first floor of Haus Firn, at the end of Musterweg, a few minutes' walk from the
village centre. Arrival is on Saturday from 4 pm to 8 pm; the [FAQ](/en/faq#arrival) says how the
keys reach you.

## By car

Follow the road up the Glemm valley into Saalbach and turn right at the church into Musterweg. Haus
Firn is the last house on the left, with its name over the door. Your parking space, if you booked
one, is the first beside the house; please leave the others free for the guests of Lärche.

## By train and bus

From Zell am See station, the regional bus up the Glemm valley stops at Saalbach Ort. From there,
walk up past the church into Musterweg: about five minutes, with a short climb at the end.

## At the house

The front door faces the road. Zirbe is the door on the right at the top of the stairs. The ski room
is on the ground floor, through the door beside the stairs, and Zirbe's rack is marked with its
name.
