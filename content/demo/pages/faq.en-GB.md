## :credit-card: Booking and payment

<details id="weeks">
<summary>How long can I stay?</summary>

We let our apartments by the week, from Saturday to Saturday, in winter as in summer. If you would
like a stay of another length, write to us.

</details>

<details id="price">
<summary>What does the price include?</summary>

The week's price is for the apartment, not per person. It includes bed linen, towels, electricity,
heating, Wi-Fi and the final cleaning. The tourist tax and a parking space come on top.

</details>

<details id="payment">
<summary>When and how do I pay?</summary>

When you book, you pay 30% of the price through the booking form, with the methods it offers. The
rest is due 28 days before arrival; if you book later, you pay the whole amount at once.

</details>

<details id="children">
<summary>Do children cost extra?</summary>

No, the price is for the apartment. Babies under 2 are not counted as guests. In Lärche and Ahorn,
children up to 14 pay no tourist tax; in Zirbe every guest counts as an adult.

</details>

<details id="cancellation">
<summary>Can I cancel a booking?</summary>

Yes, at any time in writing; an email is enough. What we charge depends on how close to arrival you
cancel, as our [rental terms](/en/terms#5-cancellation) set out. We recommend cancellation
insurance.

</details>

## :key-round: Arrival

<details id="check-in">
<summary>When can I arrive?</summary>

On Saturday from 4 pm to 8 pm. If you will be later, please let us know.

</details>

<details id="keys">
<summary>How do I get the keys?</summary>

We welcome you at the house and show you the apartment. If you arrive after 8 pm, the keys are in a
key safe by the front door; we send you its code the day before.

</details>

<details id="registration">
<summary>Why do you need an ID?</summary>

Austrian law requires us to register every guest. Please bring an ID for each adult.

</details>

## :sofa: In the apartment

<details id="wifi">
<summary>Is there Wi-Fi?</summary>

Yes, free in every apartment. The network's name and password are in the folder on the table.

</details>

<details id="ski-room">
<summary>Where do I keep skis and ski boots?</summary>

In the ski room on the ground floor, where every apartment has a rack and a boot dryer. Skis and ski
boots stay there, not in the apartment.

</details>

<details id="pets">
<summary>Can I bring my dog?</summary>

No, pets are not allowed in the houses.

</details>

<details id="house-rules">
<summary>What else should I know?</summary>

The houses are non-smoking, and quiet hours are from 10 pm to 7 am. The other house rules are in our
[rental terms](/en/terms#7-house-rules).

</details>

## :luggage: Departure

<details id="check-out">
<summary>When do I have to leave?</summary>

On Saturday by 10 am, with the apartment swept and the dishes done. The final cleaning is included
in the price.

</details>

<details id="luggage">
<summary>Can I leave my luggage after check-out?</summary>

Yes, in the ski room until 4 pm, so you can have a last morning on the slopes or the trails.

</details>

## :map: Getting here

<details id="car">
<summary>How do I get here by car?</summary>

Saalbach lies in the Glemm valley. The road into the valley turns off at Maishofen, between Zell am
See and Saalfelden.

</details>

<details id="parking">
<summary>Can I park at the house?</summary>

Yes, on a parking space beside the house, which costs extra and is paid on site. Please tell us when
you book that you need one.

</details>

<details id="train">
<summary>Can I come by train?</summary>

Yes: by train to Zell am See, then by the regional bus up the Glemm valley to Saalbach. The stop is
a few minutes' walk from the houses.

</details>

<details id="lifts">
<summary>How far is it to the lifts?</summary>

The [map](/en#location) shows the walk from each apartment to the lifts and the village.

</details>
