## When you visit the website

With every request your browser sends its IP address and technical details such as its language. Our
server needs them to deliver the page and does not store them: it keeps no access log. It records
only error messages, for example when Beds24 does not answer, and these hold nothing about visitors.
The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the website securely and reliably (Art.
6(1)(f) GDPR).

The server is rented from a hosting provider, which acts for us as a processor.

## What stays in your browser

The website remembers two settings in your browser: the season, when you switch it or open a link to
a week of another season, and the calendar view, when you choose it. It needs both to show the
website as you asked for it, so it does not ask for consent (§ 165(3) of the Austrian
Telecommunications Act 2021).

| Name     | Kind          | What it holds                                    | How long                                                    |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| “season” | Local storage | The season shown and the day it was stored       | Used for 30 days, then replaced or deleted on a later visit |
| “view”   | Cookie        | That you chose the calendar in the booking panel | 30 days at most                                             |

If you choose the list again, the website deletes the cookie. Both stay in your browser and are sent
to no one else; besides the page itself, only our server reads the cookie, to show the page in your
view straight away.

## When you book

On this demo the free weeks and prices come from a simulator, and booking stops before the form: you
enter no details and pay nothing. A website with Beds24 works like this:

The free weeks and prices come from Beds24, our booking system. When you book a week, you enter your
details in Beds24's booking form: at least title, first name and surname, email and country, and
whatever else you choose to give. The form sets a session cookie of its own (“booking”), which holds
the booking together until it is done. The payment goes through the payment provider the form shows.

The legal basis is the rental contract you make with us (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep bookings for
seven years, as tax law requires (§ 132 of the Federal Fiscal Code).

After the booking, Beds24 sends you back to our confirmation page. The address carries your name and
contact details with it. The page shows them to you once and then removes them from the address bar;
our server neither logs nor stores them.
