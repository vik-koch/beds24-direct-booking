# Rental terms

> Firn & Farn is made up: this website is a demo, and these terms are an example of what an Austrian
> landlord of holiday apartments sets out here. They are not a template for real contracts.

These terms apply to renting our holiday apartments in Saalbach. The landlord is Firn & Farn
Appartements GmbH (see the [imprint](/en/imprint)).

## 1. Booking

1. You book a week from Saturday to Saturday on this website. The contract is made once you send the
   booking form and we confirm the booking by email.
2. The person booking must be an adult and is liable for all guests of the booking.
3. An apartment sleeps at most as many guests as it has beds. Babies under 2 are not counted.

## 2. Prices and payment

1. The week's price is for the apartment, not per person. It includes bed linen, towels,
   electricity, heating, Wi-Fi and the final cleaning.
2. When you book, you pay 30% of the price; the rest is due 28 days before arrival. If you book
   later, you pay the whole amount at once.
3. You pay through the booking form, with the methods it offers.
4. A parking space costs extra and is paid on site.

## 3. Tourist tax

The municipality of Saalbach-Hinterglemm charges a tourist tax. It is €3.50 per adult per night;
children up to 14 pay none. The booking form shows it as a line of its own, and we pass it on to the
municipality.

## 4. No right of withdrawal

Accommodation for a specific period is exempt from the right of withdrawal for distance contracts (§
18(1)(10) of the Austrian Distance and Off-Premises Contracts Act). You therefore cannot withdraw
from a booking free of charge within 14 days, only cancel it as section 5 sets out.

## 5. Cancellation

You can cancel at any time in writing; an email is enough. What counts is the day your message
reaches us. We then charge part of the price:

| Cancellation                 | We charge |
| ---------------------------- | --------: |
| up to 60 days before arrival |       10% |
| 59 to 30 days before arrival |       30% |
| 29 to 7 days before arrival  |       60% |
| from 6 days before arrival   |       90% |
| no arrival                   |      100% |

If we can let the week to someone else, we charge only our costs of €50. We recommend cancellation
insurance.

If we have to cancel a booking, for example because damage has made the apartment uninhabitable, we
refund everything you have paid.

## 6. Arrival and departure

- **Arrival:** Saturday from 4 pm to 8 pm. If you will be later, please let us know.
- **Departure:** Saturday by 10 am, with the apartment swept and the dishes done.
- **Registration:** we register all guests as Austrian law requires; please bring an ID.

## 7. House rules

- The houses are non-smoking.
- Pets are not allowed.
- Quiet hours are from 10 pm to 7 am.
- Skis and ski boots stay in the ski room.
- Please sort your rubbish; the folder in the apartment shows where the bins are.

## 8. Liability

You are liable for damage that you or your guests cause in the apartment. Please report damage
straight away. We are not liable for failures we have not caused, such as those of the lifts or the
roads.

## 9. Law and jurisdiction

Austrian law applies. For consumers, the mandatory rules of their country of residence and their
general place of jurisdiction remain unaffected.
